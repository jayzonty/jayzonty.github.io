**Hi, I'm Jayzon Ty.** I am a software engineer based in Hamamatsu, Shizuoka, Japan, currently working at Arent Inc. As a CAD engineer, I help develop PlantStream, an innovative 3D CAD software for industrial plant design.

My professional background spans multiple industries:
- **CAD & Industrial Design:** Developing high-performance 3D software.
- **Game Development & AR:** Experience in PC/console development using Unreal Engine (C++), and mobile Augmented Reality using Unity and Vuforia.
- **Academia:** Former lecturer at Ateneo de Manila University, teaching Computer Graphics, Game Dev, and Computer Science.

I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from Ateneo de Manila University (Philippines) and a Master’s degree in Engineering from the Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST, Japan).
